Dreamscape Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026 (product analytics added; weekly email letter removed).

Dreamscape ("we") turns the dreams you write into woodblock-style artwork. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Dream text you write. We use it to paint your dream, to find its symbols and to write a short reflection with a question.
  • Onboarding answers and preferences: your first name, how often you remember dreams, goals, recurring themes and wake-up time. These are stored on your device to personalise the app. Your first name never leaves your device, except inside your own private iCloud storage if you use iCloud.
  • Purchase status. Subscriptions are paid through Apple. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active (see below).
  • Anonymous product-interaction events (see "Analytics" below). They never contain your dreams, your name or your email.
  • Basic technical data (for example IP address) that our server receives when it paints a dream. We use it only for rate limiting and security, and don't keep it longer than one hour.

How dream painting works

When you paint a dream, its text is sent over an encrypted connection to our server. Our server forwards it to an AI provider (APIMart) to create your artwork and its short reflection.

  • We don't store your dream text on our servers after the image and reflection are created.
  • We don't sell your dreams or use them for advertising.
  • Your dreams (text, reflections, your answers to reflection questions and painted prints) are stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, they're also kept in your own private iCloud storage, so they survive a reinstall or a new iPhone and appear on your other devices. That storage belongs to your Apple account: we can't see or access it. To remove it, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Dreamscape.

Purchases (RevenueCat)

We use RevenueCat to process subscription purchases made through Apple and to know whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives your purchase receipts and a random app user ID that the app creates. It does not receive your name, email or dreams. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Analytics (PostHog)

To understand which parts of the app help people (for example, whether onboarding is finished, a trial is started, or a first-week step is completed), the app sends anonymous product-interaction events to PostHog, hosted in the European Union.

  • Events contain only the event name and simple values such as counts, a plan name or a step number, plus technical details PostHog adds automatically (app version, device model, OS version and language). PostHog also receives your IP address when events are sent, as any internet connection does, and may use it to estimate an approximate location such as your country.
  • They never contain your dream text, reflections, answers, name or email. Screen recording and automatic tap capture are turned off.
  • Events are linked to the same random app user ID that RevenueCat uses, not to your Apple ID or any identity.
  • We use them for analytics only: never for tracking you across other companies' apps or websites, and never for advertising.

Weekly email letter (discontinued)

An earlier version of Dreamscape offered an optional weekly email letter. It has been discontinued and the app no longer asks for your email or uses Sign in with Apple. Any letter sign-ups we previously stored (email or private relay address, first name, an anonymous Apple user ID, language, time zone and consent date) will be deleted.

What we don't do

  • No selling or renting of personal data.
  • No third-party advertising trackers, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No account is required to use the app.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we schedule a daily morning reminder, a note when your weekly folio is ready and, during a free trial, one reminder before the trial ends. If you leave the app while viewing subscription options, we may also schedule one notification about a one-time discount. These are scheduled on your device and never include the content of your dreams. You can turn them off anytime in Settings.

Your choices

  • Delete the app to remove all dreams and preferences stored on your device.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • Contact us to ask about any data we may hold, or to ask us to delete it: open a request here.

Children

Dreamscape is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
